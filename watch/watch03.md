Sound piece commissioned by ICA (Institute of Contemporary Arts, London) & Instituto Cervantes for the screening of *Deprisa Deprisa* (Carlos Saura, 1981).

The film was screened inside the **“Youth” programme**, a selection of 15 films that observe and explore ways of navigating, questioning, or disrupting reality as a young adult, especially seen through the formation of groups and communities.
